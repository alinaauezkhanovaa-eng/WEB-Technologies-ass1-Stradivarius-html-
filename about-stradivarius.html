Brand History

Stradivarius, a Spanish fashion company, is known for its women's clothes, shoes and accessories. The brand is part of the Inditex Group, one of the world’s largest fashion retail companies. Its style is modern and young, with clothing for everyday wear and current trends.

The brand was established in 1994 in Barcelona, Spain, as a family-owned fashion business and later became part of Inditex. It has stores in many countries and also sells online.

A Stradivarius shop usually has separate sections for clothes, footwear and accessories. Customers can look at the products and sizes and try clothes on before buying.

This website presents the Stradivarius store in Mega Silkway, Astana: its location, opening hours, products and prices.

The brand is named after a violin maker, and a detail in its logo — a treble clef — refers to the musical origin of the name.
