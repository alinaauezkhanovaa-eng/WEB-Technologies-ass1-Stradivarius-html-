Everyday fashion in the heart of Mega Silkway

Stradivarius is a popular fashion shop in Astana's Mega Silkway shopping centre. It sells garments, footwear, bags and accessories for young women: everyday basics and pieces that make a look more stylish.

The store has a modern and welcoming atmosphere, and pieces can be mixed and matched to your own taste. Mega Silkway is convenient too: you can combine shopping with other stores, cafés and entertainment areas.

Open daily 10:00–22:00 · 1st floor, Kabanbay Batyra 62, Astana
